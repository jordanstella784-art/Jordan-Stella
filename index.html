FAQ

Questions before we start?

What services do you specialize in?

I specialize in Etsy SEO and shop optimization, Shopify store design and optimization, eCommerce strategy, graphic design, branding and product/listing presentation.

Can you work on an existing Etsy or Shopify store?

Yes. I can review an existing store and recommend or implement improvements depending on the scope and access available.

Do you also build new stores?

Yes. I can help with Shopify store setup, structure, product presentation, visual direction and launch preparation.

Can you improve my Etsy listings?

Yes. Listing work can include titles, tags, descriptions, structure, keyword direction and visual presentation.

How do I get started?

Send me an email with a short description of your store, what you want to improve and a link to the website or Etsy shop if available.
