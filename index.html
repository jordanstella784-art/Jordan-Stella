CASE STUDIES

From problem to strategy to a better customer experience.

These are portfolio-ready starter case studies. Replace the sample wording with your verified client details as we build the evidence library.

Case Study 01 • Etsy

Improving listing visibility and product presentation.

Problem

Products needed clearer keyword targeting, stronger listing structure and a more consistent presentation.

What I Did

Reviewed titles, tags, descriptions, listing structure and visual presentation to identify practical improvements.

Strategy

Align listing language with relevant customer search intent while making the shop easier to understand and browse.

ResultA cleaner, more search-focused listing structure designed to improve discoverability and customer understanding.
Case Study 02 • Shopify

Creating a stronger storefront experience for an online brand.

Problem

The store needed a more professional visual hierarchy and a smoother path from homepage to product discovery.

What I Did

Planned the store structure, product presentation, visual direction and key customer-facing sections.

Strategy

Combine clean design, clearer navigation and stronger product communication around the target customer's needs.

ResultA more cohesive storefront direction designed to build trust and make product discovery easier.
Case Study 03 • Branding

Giving a growing business a more consistent visual identity.

Problem

Brand visuals were inconsistent across promotional materials and customer-facing content.

What I Did

Created a visual direction covering typography, color, layout and repeatable creative elements.

Strategy

Use a small, recognizable design system so every touchpoint feels connected without looking repetitive.

ResultA more polished and consistent visual direction ready to carry across digital marketing channels.

Yes. I can review an existing store and recommend or implement improvements depending on the scope and access available.

Yes. I can help with Shopify store setup, structure, product presentation, visual direction and launch preparation.
